Recap

Evaluation is the habit that makes selection defensible

  • What to evaluate depends on the app type — RAG needs faithfulness first, classification needs precision/recall, code-gen needs execution.
  • Saying "I don't know" can be the correct output — refusing on out-of-corpus questions is gradeable, not a cop-out.
  • RAGAS, DeepEval, Promptfoo, and Langfuse solve different problems — pick by the job, the same way you pick a model.
  • A golden set with deliberately tricky cases beats a big pile of easy ones.
  • An eval score that doesn't block a merge is an opinion. One wired into CI is a gate.
